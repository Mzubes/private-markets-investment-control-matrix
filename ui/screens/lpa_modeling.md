# Screen: LPA / Side Letter Canonicalization

## Purpose
Model investor-specific terms and compute expected behavior for calls, fees, and distributions.

## Layout

- **Clause List**
  - Capital call mechanics
  - Fee schedule
  - Carry waterfall
  - Side letter fee waivers
  - Commitment adjustments.

- **Clause Viewer**
  - Raw clause text.
  - Structured representation (fields, conditions).
  - "Edit structured clause" action.
  - "Link to evidence" (document location).

- **Investor-Specific Modeling**
  - Investor selector.
  - Display how terms change expected call amounts and fees.
